01 / Introduction

A little about me.

Where technology, curiosity and creativity come together.

I'm Karunarathne S.M.N.A., a Software Engineering undergraduate at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), currently in my second year.

My journey began in the Arts stream, where I earned AAC at the G.C.E. Advanced Level examination and received university selection to Eastern University, Sri Lanka. I chose to follow my growing passion for technology and software engineering at SLIIT.

I'm interested in building useful digital solutions, experimenting with IoT and Arduino, and exploring visual creativity through photography and editing.
